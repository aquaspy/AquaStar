AquaStar NEO 3.x plays with the standalone `ruffle_desktop` binary from the official Artix Experimental Launcher 1.7.0 on Linux, Windows, and Apple Silicon.

That build reports `Ruffle 0.7.1-local (e4eda6fac3614ba6660e8f023295cb3cf4462575 2026-10-16)`. The copy bundled in the launcher's AppImage is licensed under the MIT license or the Apache License 2.0, at your option. The full text shipped with the binary is `artix-ruffle-1.7.0-LICENSE.md`.

Artix publishes that Ruffle build for Apple Silicon only. Intel Macs keep the official Ruffle desktop 0.7.1 universal build from https://github.com/ruffle-rs/ruffle/releases/tag/v0.7.1, which is also MIT or Apache-2.0.
